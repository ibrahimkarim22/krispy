# Archival film editions

The public collection retains **41 archival films**, alongside the 48 original Chaplin entries and five separately licensed Blender films documented in [content sources](content-sources.md). All 41 archival entries carry edition-specific U.S. rights evidence and saved in-site playback observations in [archivalMovies.catalog.json](../src/data/archivalMovies.catalog.json).

## Rights scope

Reviews are specific to the **United States** and the supplied editions. Original publication, underlying works and later audio/title/restoration additions are considered separately. Source evidence comes from the Library of Congress, Wikimedia Commons and Internet Archive. An upload or a public-domain film title alone is not treated as clearance for an unrelated edition.

Retained edition providers: Library of Congress (23), Wikimedia Commons (14), Internet Archive (4). Silent transfers and original sound editions retain the reviewed presentation. These records do not assert worldwide permission or continuing provider availability. Playback/source substitution requires a fresh edition review.

## Playback evidence

Saved October 15, 2026 observations record at least 20 seconds of playback, progressing time and decoded frames, successful pause/resume and no fatal errors for each exact source. They are historical observations, not a guarantee of current uptime. The source catalog stores the substantive evidence without generated diagnostic files. The reusable `scripts/movies/browser.cjs` tool accepts explicit candidate input for future checks; production does not read its generated outputs.

The Fighting American, The Bargain and Winning the Futurity retain their original catalog records and historical playback evidence. A later bounded October 2026 check did not confirm startup for these three sources; that observation does not establish permanent unavailability or revoke their recorded reuse basis.
